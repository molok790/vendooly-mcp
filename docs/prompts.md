# Example prompts

Talk to the assistant normally: no commands needed. Read prompts run immediately; prompts that change something produce a preview and wait for your OK.

A library of 27 ready prompts in 5 languages, split into 7 areas (15 analysis, 12 operational), is available inside the Vendooly app after login.

## Account overview

- Give me a recap of the week: revenue, orders, account health and top products.
- How much did I sell in the last 7 and 30 days, by marketplace?
- What are today's operational problems on my seller account?

## Sales and orders

- Why did my sales drop this week compared with last week?
- Which ASINs are declining versus last month?
- Show me the daily sales trend of the last 90 days for my top 5 SKUs.

## Catalog and listings

- Which of my listings are inactive or suppressed, and why?
- Check all my titles against Amazon's title length limit and list the ones to fix.
- Run a GPSR / compliance audit of my catalog for amazon.de.
- Which ASINs have no approved A+ Content?
- Rewrite the title of SKU X within the limit and show me before → after before updating it.

## Pricing and Buy Box

- Where did I lose the Buy Box this week, and on which ASINs?
- Compare my prices with the Buy Box on my best sellers.
- Estimate Amazon fees and net proceeds for SKU X at 24.90.
- Reprice these SKUs to win the Buy Box, never below my floor price. Show the preview first.

## Stock and FBA

- Which products are at risk of stocking out in FBA in the next weeks?
- How many days of cover do I have on SKU X?
- Check my FBA inbound shipments for units received less than shipped.
- Show the FBA customer returns of the last 30 days by reason.

## Amazon Ads

- Which Sponsored Products keywords spent money in the last 14 days without a single sale?
- What are my ACoS and spend by campaign over the last 30 days, and how do they compare with the month before?
- Am I paying for ads on products that are about to run out of stock?
- Which of my products that sell have no active Sponsored Products ad?
- How much of today's budget has each campaign already used?
- Pause the keywords you just found. Show me the list before doing it.
- Lower bids by 15% on keywords with ACoS above 40% over 30 days. Preview first.

## Vendor Central (if connected)

- Summarize my open purchase orders of the last 30 days.
